# Privacy Policy

5 Salats uses your location to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction.

Location data is used to provide app functionality. The app does not sell personal data and does not use advertising tracking.

If you search for a city, your search query may be sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to find the city's approximate location. OpenStreetMap's services are subject to their own privacy policy and terms.

The app may store basic preferences, such as selected city or theme, on your device.
